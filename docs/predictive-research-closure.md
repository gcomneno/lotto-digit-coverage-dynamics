# Predictive research closure

**English** | [Italiano](it/predictive-research-closure.md)


## Status

Predictive research is closed.

The active project scope is limited to:

- exact finite-state modelling;
- mathematical verification;
- descriptive comparison with historical observations;
- reproducible data maintenance and inspection.

The project does not maintain a betting or number-selection subsystem.

## What was investigated

Earlier work explored several possible historical signals, including:

- repeated-digit and twin-number patterns;
- numbers constructed from missing-digit pairs;
- single- and multi-missing-state backtests;
- frozen and sequential forecasts;
- momentum and takeoff rules;
- return-time and residual subgroup searches;
- temporal and wheel-level stability checks.

Those experiments were useful as attempts to falsify the sufficiency of the
exact-state model.

## Final conclusion

No tested predictive family produced a stable, independently useful advantage
after accounting for the exact missing-digit state and evaluating temporal
stability.

Historical deviations were generally compatible with:

- finite-sample variability;
- sparse subgroup counts;
- unstable signs across periods;
- repeated reuse of the same archive;
- selection after inspecting outcomes.

The defensible result is therefore negative:

> The retained evidence does not justify converting decimal digit coverage into
> a forward number-selection rule.

## Repository consequence

Superseded predictive scripts, tests, protocols and frozen forecasts were
removed from the publication source of truth.

This document retains the conclusion and the principal failure modes so the
same research path is not silently repeated.

The exact model, historical cycle comparison, structural analysis and anomaly
descriptions remain because they have independent mathematical or descriptive
value.

## Interpretation boundary

The closure does not prove that every conceivable external feature is
uninformative.

It establishes that the investigated coverage-derived features did not support
a stable claim under the available protocol and archive.

A rare historical event is not evidence that:

- the opposite event is due;
- a digit must compensate;
- the same anomaly will recur;
- a wheel has entered a favourable regime.

## Reopening rule

This research line should be reopened only when all of the following exist
before inspecting new outcomes:

1. a genuinely distinct mathematical hypothesis;
2. a fixed observation and scoring protocol;
3. a declared primary metric;
4. an untouched evaluation sample;
5. explicit rules for multiplicity and subgroup selection;
6. a result with practical as well as numerical significance.

The uninspected 2022 archive must not be opened merely to continue searching
after a negative result. It should be used only for a concrete question declared
in advance.
