# Historical coverage-cycle distribution

## Purpose

This report compares complete historical coverage-cycle durations with the
exact absorption-time distribution generated by the finite-state model.

The comparison is descriptive. It is not an inferential independence test and
is not a number-selection rule.

## Theoretical object

A natural cycle starts with all ten digits missing. Each wheel draw removes the
digits observed in its five numbers, represented as `01–90`. The cycle completes
when the missing set becomes empty.

For the full initial state, the exact absorption distribution has:

| Metric | Exact value |
|:---|---:|
| Mean | 3.506190 |
| Variance | 1.924821 |
| Standard deviation | 1.387379 |
| Median | 3 |
| 90th percentile | 5 |
| 95th percentile | 6 |
| 99th percentile | 8 |

## Historical archives

The primary segment is continuous:

| Year | First draw | Last draw | First date | Last date |
|:---|---:|---:|:---|:---|
| 2023 | 1 | 182 | 2023-01-03 | 2023-12-30 |
| 2024 | 1 | 209 | 2024-01-02 | 2024-12-31 |
| 2025 | 1 | 208 | 2025-01-02 | 2025-12-30 |
| 2026 | 1 | 120 | 2026-01-02 | 2026-07-28 |

The four databases contain 719 extraction dates and are merged chronologically
for each wheel.

There is no default secondary segment.

## Continuity and censoring rules

For each wheel:

1. the first observed cycle is excluded because its true beginning may precede
   the archive;
2. complete cycles after the first observed completion are retained;
3. the final incomplete cycle is recorded as right-censored but excluded from
   the duration sample;
4. year boundaries do not break a cycle when the annual archives are
   chronologically continuous.

The eleven wheels share the extraction calendar. Their cycles are pooled for
descriptive comparison but are not assumed to be statistically independent.

## Results through 28 July 2026

The continuous segment contains 2,253 complete cycles.

| Metric | Observed | Exact | Difference |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mean | 3.481580 | 3.506190 | -0.024610 |
| Variance | 1.938964 | 1.924821 | +0.014142 |
| Standard deviation | 1.392467 | 1.387379 | +0.005087 |
| Median | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| 90th percentile | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| 95th percentile | 6 | 6 | 0 |
| 99th percentile | 8 | 8 | 0 |

The observed duration range is 1–18 draws.

Across the cumulative duration distribution through draw 18:

- maximum absolute CDF difference: `0.0131885`;
- mean absolute CDF difference: `0.00186675`;
- exact probability beyond draw 18: `0.00001923`.

The empirical centre, dispersion and selected quantiles are close to the exact
benchmark. This is compatibility at the descriptive level, not proof that all
historical observations are independent samples from the model.

## Interpretation

The comparison supports the exact model as a useful description of aggregate
coverage-cycle duration over the available archive.

It does not establish:

- independence among wheels;
- absence of every possible historical irregularity;
- a predictive advantage from cycle age or past deviations;
- a profitable betting strategy.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 analyze_historical_cycle_distribution.py
```

The default outputs are:

```text
_work/historical-cycle-comparison.txt
_work/historical-cycle-comparison.json
```

The default primary databases are the complete annual archives from 2023
through 2026. Optional discontinuous archives may be supplied explicitly as a
separate secondary segment.
